good to know.

Still stuck? Ask the info tent, it's open 24 hours.

Can I bring my own camera?

Yes. Film, digital, disposable, whatever you like. Every Full Weekend pass also comes with a 27-exposure film camera, and we develop it for free if you drop it at the gate on Sunday.

Is there an age limit?

Daisy Tapes is 16+. Under-18s need a signed parental consent form at the gate. Kids under 12 come free with an adult on Sunday only.

How do I get there?

Madeliefie Farm is 75 minutes up the West Coast from Cape Town. Shuttles leave the city every two hours from Friday 10:00. Car passes are sold separately.

Is the festival cashless?

Yes. Top up your wristband online or at any top-up tent. Unused credit is refunded to your card within 14 days of the festival.

What can't I bring?

Glass, gas canisters, drones and fireworks. Sealed sunscreen, refillable bottles and folding camping chairs are fine.

Can I get a refund?

Tickets are refundable up to 1 August 2027. After that you can resell yours at face value through the official ticket swap.
