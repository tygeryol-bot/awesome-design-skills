Memory/ˈmɛm(ə)ri/ noun

  1. The faculty by which the mind stores and remembers feelings, songs and faces.
  2. Something remembered from the past. "That weekend really stuck."
  3. What you take home from Rocking the Daisies instead of a hangover.

Every festival ends. The good ones stay with you. Every October, Cloof Wine Estate in Darling becomes a place to switch off, let loose and make memories. You arrive with your bags, your plans and your phone. By Friday night, you’re probably too busy having fun to check it. You leave with songs stuck in your head, sunset photos that never quite do the moment justice, and new friends you met while waiting for water. The weekend ends, but the memories come home with you. Come empty. Leave full of moments.
