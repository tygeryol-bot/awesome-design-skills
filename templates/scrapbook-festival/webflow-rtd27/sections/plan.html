01 · travel

Getting there

  • →Cloof Wine Estate is just outside Darling, about an hour’s drive from Cape Town.
  • →Cape Town Return Shuttle: R380 per person. Add it when you build your weekend.
  • →Driving or getting dropped off? Parking, drop-off and lift-share areas will be marked on the festival map.
  • →No day passes, so plan to stay the whole weekend.

02 · packing

What to bring

Come empty. Leave full of memories.

  • ✓Your ID and your ticket (on your phone or printed)
  • ✓A tent and sleeping bag, unless you’ve booked a pre-pitched tent
  • ✓Sunscreen, a hat and a refillable water bottle
  • ✓Warm layers for the nights
  • ✓A power bank, and room in your bag for the memories

03 · camping

Camping + rules

  • ✓Camp only in the marked camping areas.
  • ✕No open fires. Cook on a gas cooker instead.
  • ✕No generators.
  • ✓Keep your camping wristband on all weekend.
  • ✓Ticket name changes: once per ticket for R100, at least 7 days before.
  • ✓If the festival is cancelled, you get a full refund automatically.
